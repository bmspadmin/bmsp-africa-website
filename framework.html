Our framework

Every BMsP program follows the same path: Discover, Develop, Create, Impact.

Stage 1

Discover

Understand yourself, your strengths and your direction.

Stage 2

Develop

Build skills, leadership and productive habits.

Stage 3

Create

Turn ideas into enterprises and projects.

Stage 4

Impact

Use what you have built to move your community forward.

The transformation journey

Change is a sequence of small shifts that add up. Each person moves through eight steps.

  1. Awareness

    Know yourself first.

  2. Knowledge

    Learn what is possible.

  3. Thinking

    Question, reason and reimagine.

  4. Skills

    Turn learning into ability.

  5. Leadership

    Take responsibility.

  6. Enterprise

    Build something useful.

  7. Productivity

    Turn effort into results.

  8. Impact

    Move others forward.
