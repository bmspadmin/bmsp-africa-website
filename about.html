Why we exist

Before people change their communities, they must first develop their own minds.

Africa's greatest resource is its people. Too many young people have ideas and energy but no space to understand themselves, build skills and test what they can create. BMsP Africa, short for Big Minds for Productivity Africa, exists to open that space, starting in Kebbi State.

What we believe

Start with the person

Self-awareness comes first. Skills and business ideas grow from knowing who you are.

Grow together

Young people learn faster in a community of peers, mentors and local partners.

Build what people need

We back ventures and solutions that create jobs and solve real local problems.

Where we are today

BMsP Africa is a founding-stage organisation based in Kebbi State, Nigeria. We have not yet run full programs. We are preparing a pilot and planning our first cohort for 2027.

Our registration is currently being formalised, and we will publish our details here as soon as it is complete. If you would like to see our plan before getting involved, ask us and we will share it.
